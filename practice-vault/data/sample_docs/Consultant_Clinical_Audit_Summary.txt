CLINICAL AUDIT & PATIENT DOSAGE SUMMARY: SPINAL DECOMPRESSION PROTOCOL

1. Clinical Audit Scope
This annual audit evaluates post-operative recovery outcomes and pharmacological analgesia regimes for 42 patients undergoing lumbar spinal decompression between January 2022 and May 2023.

2. Pharmacological Dosage Protocol
For post-operative neuropathic pain management, patient cohort X received a titrated regime of Pregabalin: initial dosage of 75 mg twice daily (BD) for 14 days, escalating to 150 mg twice daily where radicular symptoms persisted. Routine prophylaxis included prophylactic Enoxaparin sodium 40 mg subcutaneous injection once daily for 10 days post-discharge. Zero deep vein thrombosis incidents were recorded.

3. Follow-Up and Rehabilitation
All patients attended clinical review at 6 weeks and 12 weeks post-procedure. Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) verified structural decompression in 98% of cases. The private consultation fee for complex medico-legal revision reviews is £750.00 per session.
