MEMORANDUM: OFFSHORE TRUST RESTRUCTURING AND CAPITAL CAPITAL TAXATION

1. Summary of Instructions
Chambers has been instructed by Messrs. Henderson & Cross Solicitors on behalf of the Trustees of the Corringham Family Settlement (established 1998 in Jersey). We are asked to advise upon the statutory tax implications of appointing trust capital to UK-resident beneficiaries following recent legislative reforms to non-domiciled tax status.

2. Statutory Limitation Periods and Filing Dates
The applicable statutory deadline for notifying HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) of a chargeable transfer arising from the settlement is 31 January 2025. Failure to report within the prescribed statutory period attracts penalty interest under Section 98A of the Taxes Management Act 1970.

3. Restructuring Strategy
We recommend the phased distribution of unrealised offshore capital gains over three successive fiscal years. Provided the UK beneficiaries do not remit funds directly into domestic retail accounts prior to April 2025, the trust assets retain protected transitional treatment under Schedule 10. The agreed professional fee for this opinion is fixed at £4,500.00 excluding VAT.
