PRACTICE FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION & UNDERWRITER LEAKAGE AUDIT

1. Audit Objectives
Independent reconciliation of gross billed fee notes against incoming underwriter remittances and commercial insurance broker commissions for the preceding two quarters.

2. Identified Discrepancies and Fee Leakage
During the Q2-Q3 audit period, gross invoiced disbursements totalled £84,250.00. However, banking remittances received through the BACS clearing system amounted to £78,400.00, revealing an unrecovered variance of £5,850.00.
Specifically:
- Policy Brokerage Ref BRK-9912: Commission underpayment of £2,100.00 due to misapplied tier deduction.
- Medico-Legal Invoice #4401: Unsettled expert witness attendance fee of £3,750.00 overdue by 68 days.

3. Corrective Recovery Action
Formal recovery letters have been issued to the respective insurers. Practice clerks are instructed to withhold further statement clearance pending full settlement.
