Privacy notice

Last updated 13 November 2026. This notice explains, in plain terms, what personal data this website and our on-premises systems handle, and what we deliberately do not do with it.

Who is responsible for your data

Heath & City Technology Hampstead, Corringham Court, Corringham Road, London NW11 7BY, is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. Email contactus@heathandcity.com or telephone 020 4520 9114 to ask us anything about it.

What this website collects

Almost nothing. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and builds no behavioural profiles. The contact form runs entirely in your own browser: it prepares an email in your own mail application and sends nothing to any web server of ours. Server logs kept by our hosting provider record a standard request for the pages you load, which we use only to keep the site available and secure.

Google data used by the Practice Vault appliance

Where you instruct us to connect a Practice Vault appliance to your Google account, we request read-only access to Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar, together with your email address and profile. We never request the ability to send, delete or change your mail, files or events. The authorisation is held on your own machine; the data is read there and stored there. It is not uploaded to us, sold, or shared with any third party, and no data leaves the host except the ordinary requests Google requires to return your own content to you.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where the law compels us to, or where you have asked us to. Our website is served by GitHub Pages, and the site loads typefaces from Google Fonts; those providers see the standard technical data needed to answer a browser request. Client, patient and professional engagement data stays on your systems or ours, never on a public Artificial Intelligence (AI) tool.

How long we keep things

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as an ongoing conversation needs them, and routine correspondence is deleted on a yearly cycle. Appliance logs on a client site are retained for as long as that client's engagement agreement provides, and are deleted at the end of it.

Your rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 you may ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, object to its processing, or ask us to restrict it. Write to the address above and we will respond within one month. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, although we would welcome the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our practices change. The date at the top always shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be announced on the home page before they take effect for any current engagement.
