# Cold Calling Script: The Southgate Surgery

## 1. Context and Objective

1. **Target Organisation**: The Southgate Surgery, 270 Chase Side, London,
   N14 4PR (020 8440 9301).
2. **Primary Contact**: Practice Manager (or Senior General Practice [GP]
   Partner: Dr Brian Geffin, Dr Selva Nithiyananthan, Dr Sugi Sutharsan, or
   Dr Yasser Salim).
3. **Core Catalyst**:
   - The surgery's primary website provider, Silicon Practice, entered
     administration, causing sudden nationwide website outages across hundreds
     of National Health Service (NHS) practices.
   - The surgery domain (`thesouthgatesurgerynorthlondon.nhs.uk`) is offline.
   - Patient phone lines are inevitably overloaded with avoidable queries
     regarding opening hours, repeat prescriptions, and registration.
4. **Strategic Value Proposition**:
   - Local North London Information Technology (IT) consultancy (Heath and
     City Consulting).
   - Zero procurement delay: an exact, working restoration mock-up of the
     practice website is already built and ready to deploy within 24 hours.
   - Sovereign, independent static infrastructure with no software vendor
     lock-in or third-party liquidation risk.

## 2. Gatekeeper Navigation (Reception / Administration Desk)

**Objective**: Pass the receptionist professionally and reach the Practice
Manager without sounding like a generic software salesperson.

* **You**: "Good morning / afternoon. My name is Neville from Heath and City
  Consulting in North London. Could you please put me through to the Practice
  Manager?"
* **Receptionist**: "May I ask what this is regarding?"
* **You**: "Certainly. It is regarding your practice website outage following
  the Silicon Practice administration. We have already prepared a fully
  restored emergency mock-up of your website so your patients can access
  opening hours and repeat prescription guidance again today, and I need to
  share the preview link directly with the Practice Manager."
* **Receptionist**: "The Practice Manager is very busy right now."
* **You**: "I completely understand—your reception team must be dealing with
  double the normal call volume with the website down. It will take less than
  sixty seconds to pass along the staging link so the manager can review it.
  Who is currently handling the website recovery on the management team?"

## 3. Practice Manager Dialogue

### Step 1: Empathy and Direct Hook (First 20 Seconds)

* **You**: "Good morning [Practice Manager Name]. My name is Neville from Heath
  and City Consulting here in North London. I know your phone lines are
  inundated right now following the sudden collapse of Silicon Practice, so I
  will be direct: I am calling because we have already built a working,
  pixel-perfect temporary restoration of The Southgate Surgery's website."

### Step 2: Value Demonstration (No Sales Pitch)

* **You**: "Rather than calling to pitch you abstract IT services while you are
  dealing with a clinical crisis, we took your most recent archived pages—your
  Monday-to-Friday opening hours, your clinical roster with Dr Geffin and
  partners, your repeat prescription pathways, and your emergency care
  guidance—and rebuilt them on a clean, independent static server.
* "It is completely functional right now. We can have your domain re-pointed
  and live within 24 hours, which will immediately divert routine patient calls
  away from your reception desk."

### Step 3: Low-Friction Call to Action (CTA)

* **You**: "I don't expect you to make any decisions on this call. All I would
  like to do is send you the private preview link so you and the partners can
  review the live mock-up on your screen today. What is the best direct email
  address for you?"

## 4. Handling Common Objections

### Objection 1: "Our Integrated Care Board (ICB) is dealing with it."

* **Response**: "The ICB is indeed looking into long-term framework
  replacements, but as you know with NHS procurement, that typically takes
  weeks or months to finalise across all affected practices. In the meantime,
  your patients cannot check your 8:00am opening times, your measles guidance,
  or your prescription instructions. Our temporary restore acts as an immediate
  interim bridge. You can run it tomorrow while the ICB works through their
  long-term procurement, and there is zero lock-in."

### Objection 2: "We do not have the budget for an emergency website right now."

* **Response**: "We completely appreciate that practice budgets are under
  intense pressure. Because we have already completed the restoration work
  upfront, our setup fee is modest and predictable, designed specifically to
  solve this immediate emergency. If it saves your reception team twenty hours
  of repetitive phone queries this week alone, it pays for itself immediately.
  Let me send you the preview first—if you don't find it valuable, you owe us
  nothing."

### Objection 3: "Just send an email with the details."

* **Response**: "I would be delighted to. What direct email address should I
  send the private staging link to? I will include a 60-second video walkthrough
  demonstrating how your patients will access your clinic times and forms. May
  I give you a quick courtesy call tomorrow at 10:00am once you've had a
  chance to view it?"

### Objection 4: "We are simply too busy to talk."

* **Response**: "I hear you, and that is exactly why I reached out. Every hour
  your site remains down is another fifty phone calls your receptionists have
  to answer manually. I will send the link to your inbox right now so you can
  open it on your phone between clinics."
