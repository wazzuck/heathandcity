User Guide & How It Works

How to Use the London Market Email Triage System

This system automatically reviews incoming insurance correspondence, determines which department should handle it, extracts key policy details, and produces a plain-English briefing for your team. Use the three steps below to explore or triage incoming emails.

Step 1

London Market Dictionary

Top Section: Dictionary Directory

What it is for: A central directory of technical Lloyd’s and London Market insurance terminology, abbreviations, and acronyms.

  • Browse & Search: Search through the 77 active corporate terms or filter by category (e.g. Contract Documentation, Claims, Capacity).
  • Standard Definitions & Context: Review the formal market definition and practical usage example for each term.
  • How it helps: Every term in this dictionary is automatically recognised by the system to ensure incoming correspondence is understood accurately.
Step 2

Choose or Paste an Email

Bottom Left: Incoming Email Intake

What it is for: The intake workbench where new broker emails, placing slips, claims notifications, or underwriting queries are loaded.

  • Pre-Prepared Examples: Select from 12 realistic London market scenarios (or click one of the quick-load buttons) to test different email types.
  • Custom Email: Or paste any live email message directly into the form fields (plain text or email HTML).
  • Reclassify on Change: The default scenario is pre-classified on load. When you choose another scenario or edit the email text, previous results clear and a ► Reclassify button appears in the results panel to re-run the evaluation.
Step 3

Review Analysis & Routing

Bottom Right: Automated Analysis

What it is for: Instant operational intelligence and recommended actions presented across two complementary views:

  • Department Routing: Recommends the exact business department to handle the message (e.g. Risk Placement, Claims, Servicing) with an exact confidence score and category breakdown.
  • Executive Briefing: Delivers a plain-English summary, extracts key parameters (Insured, Class of Business, Reference, Limit), flags conditions/subjectivities, and highlights recognised terms (hover to view definitions).
  • View Toggle: Switch between Combined View, Routing only, or Executive Summary only.
