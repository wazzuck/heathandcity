WHAT WE SET UP

Three things, for your kind of practice

Each set-up solves one problem that generic software handles badly. Every system is set up personally by a Hampstead-based engineer with 17 years of experience building financial systems in the City of London.

BARRISTERS & KCs

Chambers and private client work

  • Find any opinion in seconds. Search twenty years of opinions, skeletons and precedents on a private system.
  • Disclosure checks. Bank statements across a large bundle are cross-checked, with gaps and unexplained transfers flagged.
  • Client ID chasers. Passports, trust deeds and source-of-wealth documents tracked, with reminders ready for your approval.
INSURANCE & STOCK BROKERS

Brokerage and trading records

  • Renewals tracked to the day. Policy renewals, underwriter queries and claims sorted by client and deadline.
  • Records ready for review. Contract notes and trade confirmations filed and kept for the period your regulator requires.
  • Chasers with a timer. Every "waiting on reply" item has a timer and a draft follow-up.
COMPOSERS & MUSIC

Royalties, commissions and contracts

  • Royalty statements, matched. Statements from collection societies and publishers, such as PRS for Music, checked against your own list of works and splits.
  • Commission deadlines. Briefs, stems, cue sheets and invoices tracked, so delivery dates stay visible.
  • Splits and contracts in one place. Co-writer shares and publishing agreements kept together and easy to find.
SURGEONS & CONSULTANTS

Clinical records and private billing

  • Medico-legal chronologies. Thousands of pages of records turned into a dated chronology, with each entry linked to its page number.
  • Underpayments found. Theatre lists, invoices and insurer remittances matched, so underpaid procedures are flagged for recovery.
  • Dictation to drafts. A post-clinic voice note becomes draft letters for GPs and insurers, ready for your sign-off.
FAMILY OFFICES & PROPERTY

Statements, leases and deadlines

  • Statements matched. Private bank, fund and managing agent documents read and checked against your own portfolio list.
  • Dates that matter. Rent reviews, break clauses and compliance certificates surfaced ninety, sixty and thirty days ahead.
  • Capital calls tracked. Each notice filed with its amount, its deadline and the source document.

Also available for accountancy and chartered surveying practices on request.

How it is built (for the technically minded)

We combine an 80% tested core with a 20% layer built for your own forms, insurers, banks or court bundles. That avoids starting from scratch each time and keeps the cost predictable.

LAYER 01 · READ-ONLY INTAKE

Safe connections

Read-only links to Microsoft 365 (Outlook), Google Workspace, and local PDF folders. Your live email is never changed.

LAYER 02 · PRIVATE PROCESSING

On-site reading and search

Two-Tower retrieval, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) fine-tuned models, and rule-based parsers that output verified records with exact page references.

LAYER 03 · DEADLINES

Deadline and reply tracker

Tracks open matters, statutory dates, and "waiting on reply" timers automatically.

LAYER 04 · SIMPLE SCREEN

One clear daily summary

A large-type dashboard, plus an optional 8:00 AM summary email listing the top priorities.
