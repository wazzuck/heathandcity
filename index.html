WHAT WE SET UP

Three things, for your kind of practice

Each set-up solves one problem that generic software handles badly.

BARRISTERS & KCs

Chambers and private client work

  • Find any opinion in seconds. Search twenty years of opinions, skeletons and precedents on a private system.
  • Disclosure checks. Bank statements across a large bundle are read and cross-checked, with gaps and unexplained transfers flagged.
  • Client ID chasers. Passports, trust deeds and source-of-wealth documents tracked, with reminders ready for your approval.
INSURANCE & STOCK BROKERS

Brokerage and trading records

  • Renewals never slip. Policy renewals, underwriter queries and claims sorted by client and deadline.
  • Records ready for review. Contract notes and trade confirmations filed and kept for the period your regulator requires.
  • Chasers that never forget. Every "waiting on reply" item has a timer and a draft follow-up.
COMPOSERS & MUSIC

Royalties, commissions and contracts

  • Royalty statements, matched. Statements from collection societies and publishers, such as PRS for Music, checked against your own list of works and splits.
  • Commission deadlines. Briefs, stems, cue sheets and invoices tracked, so no delivery date is missed.
  • Splits and contracts in one place. Co-writer shares and publishing agreements kept together and easy to find.
SURGEONS & CONSULTANTS

Clinical records and private billing

  • Medico-legal chronologies. Thousands of pages of records turned into a dated chronology, with each entry linked to its page number.
  • Underpayments found. Theatre lists, invoices and insurer remittances matched, so underpaid procedures are flagged for recovery.
  • Dictation to drafts. A post-clinic voice note becomes draft letters for GPs and insurers, ready for your sign-off.

Also available for property, family office and accountancy practices on request.

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

Four ways to work with us

How it is built (for the technically minded)

We combine an 80% tested core with a 20% layer built for your own forms, insurers, banks or court bundles. That avoids starting from scratch each time and keeps the cost predictable.
