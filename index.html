Contractual safeguards & the small print

Every engagement is underpinned by institutional contracts tailored for regulated practices. Your data is protected by enforceable legal instruments before a single line of configuration is written.

DATA PROTECTION

Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Fully compliant with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Formally establishes your practice as the Data Controller and Heath & City Technology Hampstead as the Data Processor, with strict statutory terms prohibiting any sub-processors or public cloud transfer without prior written instruction.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

Executed in advance of any preliminary review, sample document inspection, or workflow audit. Explicitly drafted to safeguard legal professional privilege, clinical confidentiality, and sensitive financial data, ensuring discussions remain strictly protected.

SECURITY CONTROLS

Technical & Organisational Measures (TOMs)

A formal schedule defining local operational boundaries: local air-gapped processing, Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256) encryption for data at rest, and memory-isolated models. Guarantees confidential records never leave your designated office hardware.

AUDITABILITY

Right to Independent Scrutiny

We operate with complete technical transparency. Your practice's appointed Information Technology (IT) consultant, cyber insurance underwriter, or compliance auditor has the full contractual right to inspect local deployment scripts, pipeline architectures, and data flow boundaries.

Standard execution drafts of our Data Processing Agreement and Confidentiality Undertaking are provided for your legal review ahead of any engagement.
